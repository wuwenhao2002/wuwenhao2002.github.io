ABOUT THE PROJECT

TCS Ontology

Machine-readable theoretical CS primitives and typed rules to help researchers and AI systems explore combinations of problems, models, methods, assumptions and properties.

Currently a curated-but-unreviewed seed covering multiple TCS areas. Blank definitions are deliberate TODOs. To propose corrections, open a GitHub pull request and include citations and precise mathematical scope.
